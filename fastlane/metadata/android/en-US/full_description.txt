Shopping List Calculator helps you plan grocery trips and see what you're spending as you shop. Make your list, enter prices as you go, and keep track of your total before you reach the checkout.

Set a budget to see how much you have left to spend, include sales tax, and save your regular shopping lists to use again next time.

Features:

• Create shopping lists and arrange items in the order you shop
• Check off items as you go and easily make changes
• See your running total, including sales tax
• Set a shopping budget and track how much is left
• Calculate costs for multiple items or items sold by weight
• Save, name, and edit reusable shopping lists
• Add a saved list to your current trip or start fresh with it
• Clear prices and checkmarks while keeping your list for another trip
• Choose your currency and how prices are displayed
• Enter prices quickly and choose whether to enter the item name or price first
• Shop with a dark interface
• Use the app offline, with no account, ads, or tracking

Saved lists remember item names, so you can enter fresh prices and quantities each trip. Your lists and settings stay on your device.
