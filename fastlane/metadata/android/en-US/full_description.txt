Shopping List Calculator is a focused, offline tool for planning a grocery trip and keeping a running total as items go into your cart.

Build a reusable shopping list in the order you move through the store. Enter a price and quantity for each item, or use weight mode for items priced by the pound. The app keeps a live subtotal, sales tax, total, and remaining budget.

Features:

• Reusable, reorderable shopping lists
• Quantity and weight-based item calculations
• Live subtotal, sales-tax, total, and budget tracking
• Completed items kept out of the way but easy to edit
• Offline operation with no account, ads, analytics, or cloud sync

Your shopping list, budget, and tax-rate settings stay on your device. The app does not request internet access or collect personal information.
