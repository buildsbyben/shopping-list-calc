Shopping List Calculator is an offline tool for planning grocery trips and keeping a running total as you shop.

Build a shopping list in store order, enter prices and quantities, and track your subtotal, sales tax, total, and remaining budget. Use weight mode for items priced per pound, kilogram, ounce, or gram.

Features:

• Drag to reorder items, with edge scrolling for longer lists
• Save named list templates; overwrite, rename, edit, or delete them
• Add saved item names to your current list or replace it with a saved template
• Quantity and weight-based price calculations
• Live subtotal, sales-tax, total, and budget tracking
• US dollar, euro, British pound, Japanese yen, and custom currency formatting
• Custom currency symbols, symbol placement, decimal places, and separators
• Direct amount entry or quick cents entry
• Name-first or price-first entry, with Enter/Next after a price to add an item
• Compact completed items that are easy to reopen and edit
• Clear trip values while keeping item names, or delete the current list
• A native dark interface with no account, ads, analytics, or cloud sync

Saved templates contain item names only, not prices, quantities, weight modes, or checked status. Replacing the current list discards its items and trip values. Loaded items start unchecked, with quantity 1 and no price. Editing or deleting a saved template leaves the current list unchanged.

Your current list, saved templates, and settings stay on your device. The app has no internet permission and does not collect personal information. Android backup is disabled; uninstalling removes the app's local data.
