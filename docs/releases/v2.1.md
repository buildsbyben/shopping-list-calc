# Shopping List Calculator 2.1

Save your regular shopping lists and reuse them on future trips.

- Save a list of item names for your next shopping trip.
- Add a saved list to your current trip or start fresh with it.
- Rename, update, or delete saved lists whenever you need to.
- Enter fresh prices and quantities each time you shop.
- Find saved lists and options to clear your trip in the updated menu.

This release also refreshes the app description, screenshots, and F-Droid cover image.

Your existing shopping list and settings stay on your device when updating.
Saved lists contain item names only, not prices, quantities, or checked status.
